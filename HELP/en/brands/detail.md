# Brand Management: Editing and Social Identity

Configure the brand's story, expertise, and social interaction settings.

---

## 🛠️ Brand Profile Configuration

### Story and Expertise
- **Description:** A text detailing the brand's mission and production philosophy.
- **Techniques Used:** List the specialized methods used in production.
- **Team Members:** Assign masters, apprentices, or staff to the public brand profile.

### Visual and Local Identity
- **High-Resolution Logo:** Upload the brand's logo for a premium look.
- **Regional Info:** City and district where the brand's workshop is located.
- **Badges:** Add achievement tags like "Sustainable Production" or "Local Artist".

---

## 🚦 Access and Following
Once a brand is set to **"Active"**, it becomes discoverable for users to follow, and its products can be showcased under its brand identity.
