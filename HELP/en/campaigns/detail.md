# Campaign Management: Configuration and Conditions

Configure headlines, rich text content, applicable products, and participant rules.

---

## 🛠️ Campaign Configuration

### Primary Info
- **Title and Description:** Slogans and detailed terms visible to users.
- **Media Management:** Upload banners or showcase images for the campaign.
- **Scheduling:** Finalize dates for automatic start and end.

### Discount and Scope
- **Discount Type:** Select whether the discount applies to the cart total or listed item prices.
- **Scope:** Limit the campaign to the entire catalog or specific category/product.
- **Participation Conditions:** Add text-based info (e.g., "Min 3 items", "For VIP members").

---

## 🚦 Activation
If a campaign is **"Active"** and today's date is within the range, the system automatically applies discounts to the basket.
