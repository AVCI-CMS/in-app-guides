# Comment Management: List View

This page allows you to approve and manage all user comments and ratings across the platform.

---

## 🔍 Feedback Oversight

### List Features
- **Approval Filter:** Quickly see and publish "Pending Approval" comments.
- **Low Rating Filter:** Filter for 1-2 star ratings to monitor customer satisfaction.
- **Module Segmentation:** Separate product comments from blog comments.

### Table Columns
- **Content:** The message written by the user.
- **Rating:** 1 to 5 stars given.
- **Related Item:** The product or article the comment belongs to.
- **User:** The person who made the comment.
- **Status:** Approved, Pending, or Rejected.

---

## ⚡ Quick Actions
- Click a row to see full comment details and write a response.
- **Bulk Approval:** Select multiple clean comments and publish them in one click.
- **Reject/Delete:** Remove spam or harrasive comments from the system.
