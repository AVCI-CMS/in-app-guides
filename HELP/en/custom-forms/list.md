# Form Management: List Overview

> 💡 **Need full step-by-step guides and workflow tutorials?**  
> Read the complete user guide at the [AVCI CMS Documentation Portal](https://doc.avcicms.com/docs/guides/modules/communication/custom-forms).

This page allows you to manage all dynamic form templates, vendor onboarding forms, and user submissions across your workspace.

---

## 🔍 Form Oversight & Table Columns

* **Form Title & Slug:** The display name and URL identifier of the form template.
* **Purpose:** The business workflow category (`MERCHANT_APPLICATION`, `BRAND_APPLICATION`, `ORGANIZATION_APPLICATION`, `SURVEY`, or `STANDARD`).
* **Submissions Count:** Total number of filled applications received from users.
* **Status:** Toggle switch to activate or pause submissions on the storefront.

---

## ⚡ Quick Actions

* **Design Form Fields:** Click any row to open the schema builder, add fields, and configure smart attribute mapping.
* **Review Applications:** Click the **"Submissions"** tab to open the applicant review workspace.
* **Create New Form:** Click the **"New Form"** button in the header toolbar to build an application or survey template.
