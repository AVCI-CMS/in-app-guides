# Dictionary Management: Editing and Linguistics

Configure term definitions, grammatical roles, and historical contexts.

---

## 🛠️ Word Configuration

### Definition and Usage
- **Primary Definition:** Accurate and clear explanation of the term.
- **Part of Speech:** Select the grammatical category.
- **Sample Usages:** Provide sentences showing the word in context.

### Etymology and Phonetics
- **Word Origin:** Historical development and source language.
- **Pronunciation:** Phonetic spelling or text guide.
- **Synonyms/Antonyms:** Link related concepts within the system.

---

## 🚦 Activation
Once a word is **"Published"**, it can be automatically hyperlinked in other content (e.g., Blog posts) or listed on the public glossary page.
