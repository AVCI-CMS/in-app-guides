# Merchant Management: Profile and Settings

Configure legal documents, bank accounts, and operational capabilities for a merchant.

---

## 🛠️ Corporate Configuration

### Legal and Financial
- **Corporate Name:** Legal registered entity name.
- **Tax Info:** ID and office (Required for trading).
- **IBAN/Bank:** Official accounts for payment settlements.

### Operational Settings
- **Location (Map):** Pin the physical store or office on the global map.
- **Team:** Assign users who have authority over this merchant account.
- **Badges:** Manage achievements like "Verified Vendor" or "Eco-friendly".

---

## 🚦 Approval Flow
1. Set a merchant to **"Active"** to allow them to trade on the platform.
2. Keep them in **"Pending"** to request additional documentation or verify credentials.
