# Merchant Management: List View

This page allows you to monitor and manage all business partners and vendor accounts across the platform.

---

## 🔍 Partner Oversight

### List Features
- **Approval Filtering:** Manage active vs. pending applications.
- **Search:** Quickly access partners via tax ID, corporate name, or trading name.
- **Map View:** Monitor the geographical distribution of your vendors.

### Table Columns
- **Logo and Name:** Visual identity of the partner.
- **Corporate Info:** Legal data (tax ID, corporate name).
- **Contact:** Primary e-mail and web address.
- **Status:** Current operational state.

---

## ⚡ Quick Actions
- Click a row to edit legal documents, bank accounts, or operational settings.
- Use the **"New Merchant"** button in the top right to start a new entry.
