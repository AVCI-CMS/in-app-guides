# News Management: Editing and Media

Configure news headlines, rich text content, and publication types (Internal/External).

---

## 🛠️ Content Configuration

### Headline and Summary
- **Primary Title:** The main headline for the news item.
- **Abstract:** A brief summary for list displays and social sharing.
- **Tiptap Editor:** Use the rich text editor for internal news articles.

### Publication Types
- **Internal:** Create full articles directly within the CMS.
- **External:** Provide a title, summary, and a link to a third-party news source.

### Media Gallery
Upload high-resolution images to the news gallery. These images can be embedded in the content or used as primary banners.

---

## 🚦 Scheduling
Set a future date to automatically publish news items on a specific day/time.
