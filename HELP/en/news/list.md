# News Management: List View

This page allows you to manage all announcements, press releases, and editorial news across the platform.

---

## 🔍 Editorial Control

### List Features
- **Type Toggle:** Separate internal (CMS-hosted) news from external news links.
- **Language Filtering:** Manage multi-language news versions independently.
- **Status Overlay:** Instantly see which items are Live, Hidden, or Draft.

### Table Columns
- **Cover Image:** Primary visual for the news item.
- **Title:** Headline of the news or announcement.
- **Category:** Editorial group (e.g., Press, Events, Industry).
- **Date:** Publication or scheduled date.
- **Status:** Current visibility state.

---

## ⚡ Quick Actions
- Click a row to edit news content, upload galleries, or configure external links.
- Use the **"New Post"** button in the top right to start a new entry.
