# Order Management: Detail and Operations

Inspect specific order items, update statuses, and orchestrate logistics.

---

## 🛠️ Order Configuration

### Product and Ticket Details
- **Item List:** View quantities and unit prices for every item purchased.
- **Participant List:** For event tickets, check who the tickets are issued for (Name, Email, etc.).
- **Sub-Orders (Marketplace):** In multi-vendor orders, each merchant operates their own sub-order with isolated items, shipping labels, and payout accounting.

### Logistics and Status Updates
- **Shipping Tracker & Barcode:** Enter the tracking number, carrier name, or generate automated shipping barcodes (Customer will be notified automatically).
- **E-Invoice Upload:** Upload seller or corporate PDF invoices directly to the sub-order.
- **Cancellation & Return Tickets:** End-user refund or cancellation requests flow into the **Ticket Module**. Operators and sellers can view linked dispute/return tickets directly in the order details drawer and approve or reject them.

### Payment Info
- **Payment Method:** Check if card, bank transfer, or wallet was used.
- **Service Reference:** Find the payment provider transaction ID for financial reconciliation.

---

## 🚦 Status Management
Notify your customers and finalize processes by moving the order from "Preparing" to "Shipped" or "Completed."
