# Order Management: List View

This page allows you to monitor all sales, ticket generation processes, and shipping activities across the platform.

---

## 🔍 Order Oversight

### List Features
- **Order Status:** Distinguish between pending, shipped, or completed orders.
- **Date Range:** Filter sales by specific dates for accounting and reporting.
- **Search:** Quickly access orders via Customer Name or Order Number (e.g., AVCI-101).

### Table Columns
- **Order No:** Unique transaction number.
- **Customer:** The person or entity who made the purchase.
- **Total:** Net payment amount and currency.
- **Type:** Physical Product or Event Ticket.
- **Status:** Current operational state.

---

## ⚡ Quick Actions
- Click a row to see order details, enter tracking numbers, or resend tickets.
- **Export (Excel/CSV):** Download the order list for offline reporting.
