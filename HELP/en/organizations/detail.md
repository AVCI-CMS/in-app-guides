# Organization Management: Profile and Legal Data

Configure legal details, financial records, and hierarchical structures.

---

## 🛠️ Corporate Profile Configuration

### Legal and Financial
- **Corporate Name and Type:** Official registered name and entity type.
- **Bank Info:** Official IBAN and banking data.
- **Contact Details:** Official email, phone, and headquarters address.

### Team and Location
- **Team Members:** Define authorized users and their roles within the organization.
- **Headquarters Location:** Pin the headquarters address on the map.
- **Web and Social:** Link the entity's digital channels.

---

## 🚦 Publishing and Authority
Once an organization profile is set to **"Active"**, it can appear as an official partner in corporate projects or collaborations.
