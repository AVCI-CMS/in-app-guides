# Finance Management: List View

This page allows you to monitor collections, refunds, and pending bank transfers.

---

## 🔍 Financial Oversight

### Transaction Monitoring
- **Method Filter:** Separate Credit Card, Transfer, or Crypto payments.
- **Awaiting Approval:** Check and manually approve payments made via Bank Transfer.
- **Refund Requests:** Finalize refund requests initiated by customers.

### Table Columns
- **Transaction ID:** Unique reference from the payment gateway (e.g., Iyzico).
- **Order:** Link to the related order number.
- **Total:** Net collected amount.
- **Method:** Which channel was used for payment.
- **Status:** Successful, Pending, or Failed.

---

## ⚡ Quick Actions
- Click a row to see payment details (Bank receipts, gateway logs, etc.).
- **Verify Transfer:** After confirming the amount in your bank account, set the payment to "Success."
