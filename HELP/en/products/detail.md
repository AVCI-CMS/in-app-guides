# Product Management: Editing and Stock

Configure product headlines, rich text content, variant options, and stock management rules.

---

## 🛠️ Product Configuration

### Content and Pricing
- **Title and Slug:** The main header and the URL path.
- **Sale Price:** Configure discounts or promotional prices.
- **Media Gallery:** Upload high-resolution images to the primary gallery.

### Stock and Inventory
- **Quantity Tracking:** Manually set stock or enable automatic tracking.
- **Variant Options:** Define sizes, colors, or materials for the item.

### Relationships
- **Brand and Merchant:** Assign the product to a specific brand or vendor profile.

---

## 🚦 Status Management
Set an item as **"Hidden"** to keep it accessible only via a direct link without showing it in the catalog, or **"Out of Stock"** to prevent automated purchases while keeping it visible.
