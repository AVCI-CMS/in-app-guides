# Product Management: List View

This page allows you to manage all items, goods, and services across the platform, including their stock and pricing states.

---

## 🔍 Catalog Oversight

### List Features
- **Stock Tracking:** Instantly see which items are "Out of Stock" or "Low Stock".
- **Vendor/Brand filtering:** View products specifically from a single brand or merchant.
- **Price Management:** Quick view of list price vs. sale price for every item.

### Table Columns
- **Cover Image:** Primary visual for the product.
- **Title:** Name of the item in the CMS.
- **Category:** Functional group of the product.
- **Stock:** Quantity available for purchase.
- **Price:** Active selling price.
- **Status:** Current accessibility state.

---

## ⚡ Quick Actions
- Click a row to edit item details, variant properties, or vendor assignments.
- Use the **"New Product"** button in the top right to start a new entry.
