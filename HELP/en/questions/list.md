# Question Management: List View

Respond to and manage product or brand questions from your customers.

---

## 🔍 Customer Question Oversight

### List Features
- **Unanswered Questions:** See pending customer questions at the top.
- **Merchant Filter:** If managing a Marketplace, monitor which merchant receives how many questions.
- **Search:** Search via message content or customer name.

### Table Columns
- **Question:** The message sent by the customer.
- **Related Item:** The product or store the question is about.
- **Date:** Time the question was sent.
- **Status:** Answered or Awaiting Response.

---

## ⚡ Quick Actions
- Click a row to answer the question and publish it.
- **Respond:** Your response is emailed to the customer and listed under the question on the website.
