# Slider Management: Slide Editing and Reordering

Add new visuals, edit text, and determine the transition order of your selected slider.

---

## 🛠️ Slide Configuration

### Visual and Text Content
- **Primary Visual:** Main image for wide screens (Desktop).
- **Mobile Visual:** Optimized image for vertical screens (Phones).
- **Title and Subtitle:** Promotional text appearing on the visual.
- **CTA (Button):** Text and destination URL for the action button.

### Ordering and Transitions
- Use drag-and-drop to change the sequence (`order`) of slides.
- **Activation:** Unpublish or hide individual slides as needed.

---

## 💡 Tip
To ensure a faster website, always add mobile visuals. The system will automatically present the correct visual based on the user's device.
