# Ticket Management: List View

This page allows you to respond to help requests (Tickets) from your customers and manage customer satisfaction.

---

## 🔍 Support Ticket Oversight

### List Features
- **Priority Level:** Display **Urgent** or **High** priority tickets at the top.
- **Status Filter:** Monitor "Awaiting Response" or "New" tickets to manage workload.
- **Category Segmentation:** Separate technical issues, return requests, or general inquiries.

### Table Columns
- **Ticket ID:** Unique reference number for the request.
- **User:** The customer needing assistance.
- **Subject/Message:** Brief summary of the issue.
- **Priority:** Level of urgency.
- **Status:** Current resolution phase.

---

## ⚡ Quick Actions
- Click a row to message the customer and resolve the issue.
- Use **"Respond"** to send immediate notifications to the customer.
- **Change Status:** Set tickets to "Waiting Customer" when needing info, and "Closed" when resolved.
